How it works
  • Champion pieces are the core five. Add-ons add profit and trades; Big Dip Short and Midday Follow are new in v5. Up to 5 contracts can be open at once, and the pieces often point the same way.
  • One contract per piece (MNQ by default). Every position is flat by 15:55 ET. Times are US Eastern.
  • Opening Drive has 3 upgrades: it also trades when the 10:00 close is 0.2 x range past the overnight high/low, the stop is capped at 0.6 x range, and it re-enters once per day on a new day extreme (until 15:30).
  • Safety: daily loss stop, max drawdown stop, max contracts, optional news pause. Paper mode is the default; Live needs typing LIVE.
  • v5 rules: shorts skip days when the overnight range already took the prior-day low; First FVG only trades with the morning move; ORB retest targets 3R; Small Dip trades any calm open with a 1.5% stop. Each can be switched back to the old rule in Bot settings.
  • Retired (tested, no edge, removed): SD fade, AMD AM, AMD PM, Nowick wickless candles. Not in the bot: Gold prior-day-break (edge faded in 2026, paper-trade only), ES/YM (Opening Drive is NQ-specific).
